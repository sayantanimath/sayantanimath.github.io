Research

PhD Work

  • (with J. D. Clifton, Z. Walsh) The unbreakable quasi-graphic matroids

    Quasi-graphic matroids generalize both frame and lift matroids, which in turn generalize graphic matroids. This paper gives a complete characterization of the 3-connected unbreakable quasi-graphic matroids. A connected matroid is unbreakable if it remains connected after contracting any flat. The class of quasi-graphic matroids have been structurally studied by Bowler, Funk, Slilaty.
